# Style
A clear recorded walkthrough, not a promotional montage. Use the actual terminal palette, a dark neutral surround, and restrained white labels. No stock footage, fake typing, animated code output, music or SFX. Show real approval text long enough to read. Use a monospace face already licensed on the host; retain its provenance. Terminal text should read at 1080p without full-screen zoom. Prefer 24–28 px equivalent text and fewer visible rows over tiny full-desktop capture.

Keep motion purposeful: one crop to the input, one to code, then output. Never crop away a failure or approval qualification. Show “Wait shortened” when time is removed; preserve all interaction in the raw recording. Real long-running operations may require changing the proposed shot timing within the 300-second cap. Do not speed up speech to force the plan.

Voice is calm, conversational English. Explain the action before the output. Pronounce Kujo consistently with existing brand voice evidence; listen to the generated sample. ElevenLabs V4 requested; supported model ID and chosen voice remain unresolved. No music helps users read and avoids extra rights/cost scope. Transcript is provisional until the actual recording exists.

No external reference supplied or researched. These are general readable-screencast principles plus first-party VideoOps standards, not imitation of another creator.
